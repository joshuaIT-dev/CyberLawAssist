# 404 design notes

Direction: Oversized text-based 404 in city green on the original mint background.

Subject inventory: CyberLawAssist, Muntinlupa City, Public Attorney office, city green, action blue, office case records, the Guest/User/Admin access system, Tunasan office contact, and the need to return to legal information without treating an error as an official case result.

Argument: a genuine 404 status means the resource was not found; the oversized status graphic communicates it, the working Home link recovers navigation, and the office telephone provides a separate real contact.

Idea: large, clean 404 typography leads into a short explanation and direct Home / Guest actions. Live action: return Home or open the Guest workspace. No invented statistics, testimonials, official seal, stock photographs, or additional marketing sections.

The 404 is native text and scales at all screen sizes. Existing server-side 404 handling is retained. Browser inspector and screenshot checks were not run.
