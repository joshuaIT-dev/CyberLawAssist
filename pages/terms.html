1. What CyberLawAssist provides

CyberLawAssist provides general cyber law information, an offline chatbot, a decision guide, preparation checklists, and communication with the Muntinlupa City District Office - Public Attorney's Office.

Using the chatbot or guide does not file a complaint, schedule an appointment, determine eligibility for assistance, or create an attorney-client relationship. An office representative must confirm any official next step.

2. Guest, User, and Admin access

  • Guests can use public information and the chatbot. Guest conversation history stays in the current browser profile.
  • Users register through the public signup form and can save personal records and contact the office through Live support.
  • Admins are created by existing administrators and access the office workspace using their assigned admin number and password.

Use only the workspace assigned to your account. Do not try to access another person's account or records.

3. Your account and responsibilities

Use accurate contact details, keep your password private, and log out when using a shared device. The optional phone field can be left blank. An admin number identifies an administrator; it does not replace the administrator's password.

Never submit passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, payment-card details, or unrelated sensitive information in chat. Contact the office if you suspect unauthorized account access or need help recovering your account.

4. Acceptable use

Use the system to seek information, prepare for an office visit, or communicate about a concern. Do not impersonate another person, harass staff or other users, distribute malware, interfere with the service, or attempt to bypass access controls.

Describe incidents honestly and share only information needed for your concern. When describing harmful material, avoid uploading or repeating unnecessary personal details about other people.

5. Chatbot and decision guide limitations

The chatbot retrieves answers from local English, Tagalog, and Taglish question-and-answer files. Answers can be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular situation. The decision guide organizes the information you select; it does not decide guilt, legal liability, a case outcome, or entitlement to representation.

Check linked sources and ask office staff to confirm information relevant to your circumstances. Do not treat a chatbot reply as an official decision or assurance that a complaint has been received.

6. Live support and urgent situations

Live support is a conversation between a registered User and office administrators. Guests do not have Live support access. Messages are saved on the office server so the conversation can continue.

Replies depend on staff availability. This feature does not promise an immediate response or continuous monitoring and is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service directly.

7. Checklists, exports, and office records

Your saved checklist is a preparation aid. Word exports reflect saved data and the selected reporting period at the time of generation. A downloaded document is not proof that an appointment, complaint, or legal representation has been approved.

Keep downloaded or printed copies secure. Changing or deleting an online record does not alter copies that have already been downloaded, printed, or included in an earlier backup. Physical intake records are recorded separately by office staff.

8. Privacy and acknowledgement

Read the Privacy Policy for the information collected, who can access it, how browser storage works, and how to contact the office about privacy requests.

Public registration requires agreement to these Terms and acknowledgement of the Privacy Policy. The policy version and time of acknowledgement are saved with new User accounts. Acknowledging a privacy notice does not authorize unrelated uses of your information.

9. Availability and changes

The local server must be reachable for login, saving account records, Live support, and the chatbot. Public file caching can help load interface files; it does not let you sign in or submit messages while the server is unavailable.

The service may be interrupted by maintenance, connectivity problems, or software errors. If a save reports an error, review the message and retry before assuming the action was completed. Features and information may change as the project is maintained.

10. Account restrictions and help

Administrators can disable User accounts and reset passwords after checking the account owner's identity. Permanent User deletion requires administrator confirmation and also removes the User's saved personal activity, including the associated Live support conversation. Office records with a retained creator reference may prevent deletion.

Contact the office if you believe an account restriction is an error, need a record corrected, or want to ask about account deletion. These Terms do not remove rights provided by applicable law.
